# Chime Cut

This is a browser-based audio clip maker. It works without a server: your audio stays on your device.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html`, or upload this folder to any static website host.

## Use it

1. Select an audio or video file.
2. Pick where the clip starts and how long it should be. The available range comes from the full file duration; you can enter times such as `5:00` directly.
3. Use the audio waveform to spot quieter and louder sections. Tap or click it to set the start, then preview the moving playhead.
4. Preview and save the finished WAV file.
5. On iPhone, import the WAV into GarageBand and share it as a ringtone. iPhone ringtones are limited to 30 seconds.

## Notes

- Audio and video selections export as WAV. Video sound is captured while the selected section plays, so keep the page open until the save finishes.
- Video files show their full timeline; a sound waveform is shown for audio files.
- It does not upload or retain the audio you select.
